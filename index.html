About AquaFind

AquaFind is a Design Thinking project developed to make locating drinking-water points easier. It provides a map, location-based distance estimates and a complaint reporting interface.

The current version uses demonstration water-point data. Actual water availability and water quality must be verified before visiting or drinking.
